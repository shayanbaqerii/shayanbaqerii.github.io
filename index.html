Witteveen+Bos project portal

Full-stack developer and Scrum Master. Saxion client project with a client and three stakeholders at Witteveen+Bos. Team of six, nine weeks in two-week sprints, graded 9.

Project information at Witteveen+Bos was spread across several platforms, with no standard process and little overview of running projects. We built one portal for the whole project lifecycle: requests, proposals, an approval workflow, execution, risk management, and evaluation and closure, with notifications and team management on top. I worked across all of it, from the database schema to the React frontend.

  • Built JWT login with role-based access in Spring Security.
  • Restructured the Flyway migrations so the schema could keep changing safely.
  • Wrote integration tests against a real PostgreSQL with Testcontainers.
  • Ran the Scrum events: sprint planning and reviews every two weeks, and regular meetings with the client and stakeholders. Features shipped incrementally through merge requests and GitLab CI/CD.
  • React 18
